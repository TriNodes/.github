# TriNodes Governance

## Purpose
This document defines how decisions are made across the TriNodes organization.

---

## Roles

### Maintainers
- Approve PRs  
- Manage releases  
- Enforce standards  
- Oversee repository health  

### CODEOWNERS
- Mandatory reviewers for protected areas  
- Responsible for quality and consistency  

### Contributors
- Submit PRs  
- Follow standards  
- Participate in discussions  

---

## Decision Making

### Technical Decisions
Made by maintainers and CODEOWNERS.

### Organizational Decisions
Made by TriNodes leadership.

### Disputes
Resolved by maintainers through consensus.

---

## Release Governance
- All releases follow SemVer  
- PRs require bump labels  
- Automated release workflows handle versioning  
- Breaking changes require maintainer approval  

---

## Workflow Governance
- All workflows must use reusable templates  
- Dangerous permissions require justification  
- Security Suite Enterprise must remain enabled  

---

## Standards Enforcement
All repositories must follow:

- STANDARDS.md  
- CONTRIBUTING.md  
- SECURITY.md  
- CODEOWNERS  
- CI/CD requirements
